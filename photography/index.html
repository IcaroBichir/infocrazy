Behind the Lens

Photography is where my eye for content started. Before scripts and shot lists, there were compositions, light, and the discipline of making a single frame tell a story. That foundation shapes everything I create for brands today.

I gravitate toward travel, lifestyle, architecture, and street photography — warm tones, cinematic framing, and an instinct for the moments most people walk past.
